00 / Statement Artist Statement

A graph, when it is set in motion, becomes a swarm — links stretching, points colliding, structure emerging from pressure.

I am an Iranian-born artist working at the intersection of classical ornament and generative systems. My work begins with a simple observation: the network — points and their links — has always carried motion. Roads, constellations, weaving drafts, trade routes. A graph is not a picture of stillness; it is a picture of relationship under tension.

Brownian Graphe is a seed-based generative system that translates that tension into code. Each composition is built from a small set of points — some connected, some free — bouncing inside the frame with simulated Brownian motion. Where points collide, they swap velocities and invert colour. Where they approach, the lines between them shift in brightness by distance.

The number of points, the density of the graph, the boundary buffer, the ball diameter, and the background gradient are all derived from a single numeric seed. The plate is live: you may click anywhere on it to add a new point, and the graph will rewire itself around it.

On AI tools — where I encountered technical obstacles, I turned to AI tools for debugging and code optimization. Every structural, aesthetic, and conceptual decision remained my own.

01 / Seed

Variation

A numeric seed initializes palette, point count, graph density, boundary buffer, and ball diameter. Each composition is regenerated and documented as a distinct, traceable output.

02 / Tension

Geometry

A symmetric graph matrix links each point to a random subset of the others. Points move with Brownian velocity, bounce at the boundary, and swap state on collision — a visual vocabulary drawn from network physics.

03 / Surface

Application

The resulting composition becomes a surface-design asset — explored across garments, textile prints, wallpaper and other pattern-driven applications.
